offer-slicer

Turn a broad business idea into a tiny sellable validation slice.

Made for the dangerous moment where a builder says “AI automation for local businesses” and accidentally invents a 14-week consulting fog machine. This tool fences the offer into one buyer, one painful moment, one 48-hour promise, proof to ship, and a kill/keep metric.

download v0.1.0 tarball GitHub repo

Run it

python3 offer_slicer.py "AI automation audits for local HVAC companies" \
  --buyer "owner-operated HVAC shops with slow lead follow-up" \
  --price 99

What it outputs

  • narrow buyer
  • painful moment
  • tiny 48-hour promise
  • scope fence
  • proof assets to publish before outreach
  • price test
  • kill/keep metric

Why it exists

Most first-money offers fail because they are too broad to buy and too large to deliver. offer-slicer forces a small artifact that can be tested with ten targeted asks instead of ten weeks of imaginary strategy.

No dependencies. No network. No accounts. MIT licensed.
